Built for all hair types.

From pin-straight to tight coils, Tressana covers the full spectrum. Your hair, your rules.

Straight
Type 1A to 1C
Fine to coarse, no natural curl. Needs volume and lightweight care.
Wavy

Coily
Type 4A to 4C
Coils, zig-zags, and tight patterns. Incredibly versatile, needs deep moisture.

Why we're building this

Hair tech has a representation problem.

The hair care industry generates billions, yet most of its technology was built around straight textures. Type 4 coily hair, worn by millions, remains an afterthought.

We started Tressana because we were tired of the guesswork. Tired of product graveyards. Tired of salon anxiety. So we combined real hair science with AI to build something that actually sees you.

Tressie analyses your porosity, density, scalp health, and strand pattern to deliver care that's genuinely personal, not another one-size-fits-all recommendation engine.
